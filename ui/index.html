Eat for a sustainable comeback

The earlier plan cut rice too abruptly and gave meal totals that were too certain. Start by measuring your actual portions for three days. Cooked rice weight, dry soy weight and edible chicken weight are different measurements.

Starting guide: around 2,000 kcal/day

An estimate, not a measured requirement. Aim toward 120–140 g protein where practical. Review your weight average, hunger and training after 2–3 weeks. A reasonable personal target is about 0.3–0.6 kg/week; early water loss is not all fat.

A practical meal structure

  • After training: 2 eggs, plain curd or milk, and fruit; add oats or roti if needed.
  • Rice-free lunch: 40–50 g dry soy chunks, cooked according to the packet; 100–150 g boiled chickpeas; cucumber/carrot and other vegetables; plain curd if available.
  • Snack: fruit with curd or milk. Plan it before you become extremely hungry.
  • Dinner: a measured rice portion, vegetables and dal, plus chicken on your usual 3–4 days/week; soy/tofu on other days.

These are meal building blocks, not a calorie-verified menu. Check packet labels and measure your share of oil. Two eggs alone do not cover your protein target.

Make rice reduction gradual

If 2 kg cooked rice/day is accurate, reduce your measured baseline by roughly a quarter first while adding protein and vegetables. Reassess hunger, then reduce again. Don't jump straight to 150 g for the whole day. Carbohydrates can support your workouts.

When chicken triggers overeating

Serve one plate before sitting down; put the pot away. Eat slowly and wait before seconds. If still hungry, add a planned portion of protein or vegetables. Don't skip your next meal to compensate.

Recovery & everyday care

Vegetables

2–3 carrots or cucumbers can fit into a varied diet if comfortable for your digestion. Size matters; don't force huge amounts or replace complete meals with salad. Wash produce and increase fibre gradually.

Sleep

Wake at 7:45 AM. Start winding down around 10:45 PM; aim for lights out around 11:30–11:45 PM. Move bedtime earlier by 15–30 minutes over several nights if necessary.

Morning grooming

Brush teeth, shower after training, change sweaty clothes, comb hair. Use gentle cleansing, moisturiser as needed, and sunscreen for outdoor exposure.

Evening reset

Brush teeth, clean between teeth, prepare clothes and desk, then five quiet minutes of meditation. Wash your scalp according to sweat/oil and keep pillowcases clean.

How to adjust

Compare weekly average weights rather than single readings. If there is no downward trend for 2–3 consistent weeks, modestly reduce portions or add walking. If strength, energy or hunger worsen markedly, ease the deficit and extra activity.
